Privacy Policy — Kerf: Wood Puzzle

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Developer: VOENGAMES

In short: Kerf has no accounts and we do not collect personal data ourselves. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Who we are

Kerf: Wood Puzzle ("Kerf", "the app") is developed and published by VOENGAMES, an independent developer. Questions about this policy: voenngames@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device only: completed levels and stars, settings (music, sound effects, vibration, theme, language), tutorial progress and your daily hint count. This data is not sent to us or to any server, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

The app does not ask you to sign in, and it does not use your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Kerf is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch for an extra hint, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To deliver ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads (for example views and taps) and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see them, and prevent fraud and abuse. Where you have given consent, ads may be personalized. Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app shows Google's consent form, where you can accept or decline personalized ads and data use. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • The rewarded ad for hints is always optional.

5. Children

Kerf is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not store any personal data on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained and deleted according to Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device ad settings.

7. International transfers and security

Google may process advertising data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards described in its policies. Data between the app and Google is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or KVKK in Türkiye), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google; you are also welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example if new services are added). The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
