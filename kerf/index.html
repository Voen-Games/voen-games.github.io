Kerf

A calm woodworking puzzle for Android.

About the game

A plank slides into a carved board and gets stuck. Draw a saw line to cut it exactly where it catches — straight, at 45°, or along a curve — then fit every offcut into the board's pockets. Nothing goes to waste.

  • 100 handmade levels across five packs, from Apprentice to Master
  • Curved cuts, wood knots the saw can't pass and limited-cut challenges
  • Calm music, light and dark themes, English and Turkish

Oyun hakkında

Kalas oyuğa kayar ve takılır. Takıldığı yerden testereyle kes — düz, 45° ya da kavisli — sonra kopan her parçayı tahtadaki ceplere yerleştir. Hiçbir parça boşa gitmez.
